# The Overseer - Personal Accountability System


A personal accountability system that combines hardware Raspberry Pi with webcam and Bluetooth speake and software (monitoring scripts on your Mac/Windows). Every morning at 9 AM, the Pi's webcam checks if you're sitting at your desk. If it doesn't detect your face, it plays an alarm through a speaker. Even after you sit down, your Mac/Windows computer remains locked (websites blocked) until you submit proof of completing your daily task. If you leave your desk for more than 5 minutes, the alarm triggers again. The system verifies your submission isn't fake through a combination of screenshot analysis, and timestamp validation. Everything runs locally on your home network for privacy, with the Pi acting as the enforcer and your Mac as the monitored device.
